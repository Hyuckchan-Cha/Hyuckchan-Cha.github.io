I am an undergraduate student in Physics Education at Seoul National University, with a combined minor in Brain-Mind-Behavior. My goal is to understand human learning, cognition, and memory in the brain through computational modeling, and to develop methods that accurately measure ability and support human learning.

I am a research intern at the Data Science Laboratory at SNU, advised by Prof. Junghyo Jo. My current work is on item response models that combine a latent ability with a graph of direct item-to-item dependencies: how to estimate them when test booklets leave many responses missing, and what limits arise when ability is represented explicitly alongside such a graph.

More broadly, I am interested in computational approaches to the brain and cognition, in measuring human learning ability, and in how humans and large language models differ in representation, inference, and memory.
